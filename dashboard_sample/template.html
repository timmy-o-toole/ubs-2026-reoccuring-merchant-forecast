Client risk monitor

Will a client's recurring payments over the next 30 days arrive on time? Every two weeks, each client gets a risk index from 0 (every payment on time) through 50 (surely late) to 100 (surely missed), together with the behaviour that moved it.

Pick a client from the list, then hover or click any date in the charts. Arrow keys step through dates.
